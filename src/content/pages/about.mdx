      - title: Career
        content: >
          Hark Singh works as a Software Engineer I at SSW, with a primary
          technical focus on .NET, Azure, Flutter, and Angular, and a growing
          emphasis on automated testing using Playwright. His work includes web
          development, cloud-based systems, and DevOps-related workflows.


          Hark's path to joining SSW is closely associated with persistence and
          direct engagement. In Jan 2024, while returning from university, he
          noticed SSW’s well-known **“404 Developer Not Found - We Are Hiring”**
          signage outside the company’s office. Curious and open to
          conversation, he decided to walk in and speak with the team.


          During this visit, he met
          [Wicksy](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/matt-wicks/) who took the time
          to speak with him. They explained SSW’s culture, learning environment,
          and introduced him to [SSW FireBootCamp](https://firebootcamp.com/),
          the company’s intensive graduate and work-experience program.
          Following this conversation, Hark was advised to reach out to
          [Penny](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/penny-walker/), who manages
          initial applications.


          Although Hark was unable to secure a work experience position in 2024,
          he continued to follow up consistently. Every 3 months, he checked in
          with Penny to ask about any new opportunities, maintaining regular and
          respectful communication without giving up.


          After nearly a year of persistence, in January 2025, Penny recognised
          Hark's consistency and determination and offered him an opportunity to
          join SSW FireBootCamp, which he commenced in April 2025.


          During FireBootCamp, Singh was mentored by senior solution architects,
          including [Cookie](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/luke-cook/),
          [Isaac](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/isaac-lombard/) and
          [Lewis](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/lewis-toh/), and supported by
          induction buddy, and seniors such as
          [Toby](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/toby-churches/),
          [Marina](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/marina-gerber/),
          [Uly](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/ulysses-maclaren/),
          [Adam](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/adam-cogan/), and Wicksy. This
          environment played a key role in shaping his first 5 months in the
          professional world of software engineering.


          As part of the evaluation process, Hark was later invited to complete
          a coding challenge, working closely with Wicksy and [Dan
          Mackay](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/daniel-mackay/). This was
          followed by an in-depth code review and technical discussion, where
          decisions, trade-offs, and design choices were openly discussed.


          Following these evaluations, and based on collective trust from
          leaders involved in the process, a decision was made to offer Hark a
          full-time role at SSW. He now works proudly within an environment
          where ideas are welcomed, learning is encouraged, and close
          collaboration is part of everyday work.

      - title: Early life and education
        content: >
          Hark Singh was born in **[Amritsar,
          India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amritsar)** into a family of
          teachers. Both of his parents worked in education, which supported a
          strong academic foundation from an early age. He largely self-studied
          through school until Year 10 and did not rely on external tutoring.


          During his school years, Hark actively participated in **theatre,
          sports, public speaking, and cultural activities**, with a strong
          interest in drama. His involvement in theatre played a key role in
          developing communication skills, maturity, and an ability to
          understand different perspectives.


          <ImagePair leftUrl="/uploads/about/hark-year1-play-cinderella.webp" leftAlt="Hark in class 1 play Cinderella" leftCaption='Hark in the school play "Cinderella", Class 1 Annual Function (2009)' rightUrl="/uploads/about/hark-year1-play-v2.webp" rightAlt="Hark is happy when he found Cinderella for the Prince" rightCaption="Hark is happy when he found Cinderella for the Prince" />


          From approximately **Year 6 to Year 10**, Hark aspired to join the
          **[Indian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Navy)** and was
          deeply committed to that goal. Due to medical eligibility requirements
          related to eyesight, he was unable to pursue this path further.
          Following this, he gradually redirected his focus toward **computer
          science and technology**.


          His interest in computing was reignited after engaging with **[CS50:
          Introduction to Computer Science](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/)**, a
          foundational computer science course offered by **[Harvard
          University](https://www.harvard.edu/)**. The course reconnected him
          with early exposure to computers at home, including software, games,
          and basic productivity tools. This experience shaped his decision to
          pursue formal studies in computer science.


          With the support of his family and **Herry** Uncle, Hark later moved
          to **[Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia)** to
          continue his education and professional development in software
          engineering.
