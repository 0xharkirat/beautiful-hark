      - title: Career
        content: >
          Hark works with C#, .NET, Azure, Playwright for automated testing, and
          Flutter for mobile apps. At SSW he is currently working on
          [EagleEye](https://ssweagleeye.com). He also does developer relations
          for projects including [TinaCMS](https://tina.io/), and makes videos
          with the [SSW TV
          team](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/?role=Marketing-%26-Video) for the
          [SSW](https://www.youtube.com/@SSWTV) and
          [TinaCMS](https://www.youtube.com/@TinaCMS) YouTube channels.


          In January 2024 Hark passed the SSW Newcastle office on the tram to uni
          most mornings, without ever really looking at it. There is a permanent
          sign on the side wall: "Error 404 - Developer Not Found, we are
          hiring". He read it properly for the 1st time while waiting for his
          tram home, had 8 minutes, and went in.


          He met [Matt Wicks](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/matt-wicks/) and
          [Seth Daily](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/seth-daily/), who explained
          what SSW does. Hark told them he was a final year student, keen to get
          hands-on experience in the software engineering world. They pointed him
          at FireBootCamp, SSW's full-stack training program, and told him to
          email the hiring manager, [Penny
          Walker](https://www.ssw.com.au/people/penny-walker/).


          He emailed her from the tram, less than 10 minutes after meeting them.


          Penny replied the same day. However, spots were full, and SSW only
          takes 1 or 2 interns a year. So Hark was persistent in his emailing,
          roughly every 3 months, for a year. In January 2025 Penny told him to
          try again in March. He did, and she gave him a chance.


          He passed a communication challenge and a coding challenge, and started
          as an intern in April 2025.


          At the end of the internship, 5 months later, he sat another full-day
          coding challenge. SSW hired him full time as a software engineer.
      - title: Move to Australia
        content: >
          Hark arrived in January 2022, as pandemic restrictions were easing. He
          spent his 1st year in Sydney with his uncle Herry, his father's best
          friend, and Herry's family, who took him in like their own son. He
          worked with Herry in his pizza shop, and the family played UNO most
          nights. Those were good days.


          In 2023 he moved to Newcastle, transferring his Bachelor of Computer
          Science from Western Sydney University to King's Own Institute, where
          the same degree cost about half as much. He was working in an Amazon
          warehouse at the time, and his 1st trip to Newcastle was on an Amazon
          truck, as a passenger.
      - title: Early life
        content: >
          Hark was born in [Amritsar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amritsar),
          India. Both of his parents are teachers, and he got through school
          without a tutor.


          He did a lot of theatre, usually with the same group of friends, and
          participated in most school activities and sport alongside it. His 1st
          time on stage was a school play based on Cinderella, in 2009.
